# Billing: dues, advances, final bills and bill dates

Scope: the auto-invoice machinery above the invoice (`crm.billing_due`, kinds `period` / `advance` / `final`): bill dates (Q042, Q071), go-live month (Q027), the advance and final bill (0320), advance-starts-work and its chasing (0322), safeguards (0321), Raise invoice now (0354), the ad-hoc advance (0363-0364), the client's own tax quarter. Invoice numbering, GST, TDS and round off are `invoicing-and-gst.md`; revenue figures are `revenue-and-reports.md`; the work-vs-bill read is there too.

Phase 1 (0318/0319, `bib/prompts/auto-invoice/PLAN.md`) writes a `crm.billing_due` per period of recurring work and a person raises the invoice.

## When a bill is dated

- **Arrears: billed on the period's LAST day (Q042, 0339, 2026-10-04).** Ashish: "1st November ko na bana kar 31st October ko hi banao". `scheduleFor` sets an arrears schedule's first bill on its first period's last day; `billingDues` bills every period of such a schedule on its own last day, read off the period (never "first plus N months": 30 Nov plus a month is 30 Dec). The sweep makes it that day on the issuing company's calendar. The invoice is still dated and numbered at Issue (0322), so a bill made 31 Oct and issued 2 Nov is a 2 Nov invoice. 0339 moved arrears schedules already set to the 1st back one day; an advance, a letter's own date and a day a person typed are untouched; bills already written keep their day. The letter prints "billed at the end of each month".
- **A yearly bill on its filing's due date (Q071, 0354, 2026-10-05).** Ashish (4 Oct): (a) most yearly work is statutory: bill ON the due date (audit 30 September, GSTR-9 31 December, UAE corporate tax nine months after the client's year end); other yearly work on a day set when the letter is finalised; (b) after a job or task closes, offer to raise the invoice; (c) monthly and quarterly stay on the period's last day (Q042); (d) UAE VAT on the client's own VAT quarter (below).
  - **Which filing** (`statutoryBillDay`): for each YEARLY billing period, the client company's yearly filings (a monthly or quarterly one never dates a yearly bill) of the rules the service carries (T6.16 lineage, or generated for the engagement: CA#11's predicate) whose due date falls INSIDE the billing year, on or after the first day served. Several (audit, ITR, AOC-4): the **EARLIEST** (user, 2026-10-05: the first deliverable). The audit due 30 Sep 2026 dates the bill for the year April 2026-March 2027 ("the filing's own year" would put a new client's first bill 18 months out). Withdrawn filings and filings due before the work began never count.
  - **Only the default is replaced.** `billingDues` reads filings only for a 12-month schedule on the period's last day (arrears). A letter that chose "start of the year" or its own day (`on_date`) keeps it: a person's choice beats a derived one. No filing: the year's last day, as before. Written bills keep their day.
  - **The bill names the FILING's year** (`billLabel`): the audit billed 30 Sep 2026 is "Statutory audit, FY2025-26" on the line (user, 2026-10-05: CA firms invoice an audit that way); `period_start`/`period_end` stay the billing year (the anti-join key). `crm.billing_due.dated_by_filing_id` (0354, composite FK, period bills only) records which filing set the day; ledger `billing_due.created` carries it.
  - The sweep skips a subject only when neither its schedule's first date nor any filing's due date has come, and cuts periods from `billing_from` (not `first_invoice_on`). Work vs Billing places a filing under the bill it dated; `expectedBill` takes the same filings. The letter PDF says a yearly arrears fee is "billed on the due date of its annual filing, or at the end of each year", an `on_date` one "billed every year from <day>".
- **Raise invoice now** (`usecase/billing/early.ts`, `EarlyBiller`): `GET /billing/bill-early` (`jobId`, `workItemId`, `partyId`, `mine`; `party:read`) lists periods whose work is done (a period job done; or the filing that dates the bill done: its job done, task completed, or marked filed; `infrastructure/postgres/done-work.ts`) and whose bill date is still ahead; `POST /billing/bill-early` (`subjectKey` `e:`/`g:`, `periodStart`; `party:write`) writes that period's bill dated today on the issuing company's day. It is the sweep's own bill made sooner: the (subject, period) unique key means never a second bill; a second press is `BILL_EARLY_REFUSED` (409), as is work not done. Ledger `billing_due.created` with `how: billed_early`, `plannedBillOn`, `workKind`, `workId`. Auto-billing off: nothing offered, `AUTO_BILLING_OFF`. `mine` = the bill's manager (`billingManagerFor`).
- **A quarterly service on the client's own tax quarter (Q071 d, S7, 2026-10-05).** A quarterly subject whose service carries an AUTHORITY-ASSIGNED rule (0359) is cut on the quarter the client company recorded for that rule's tax (`rp.tax = upper(module)`, not cleared): `ASSIGNED_QUARTER_MONTH` (`infrastructure/postgres/assigned-quarter.ts`) is ONE SQL fragment read by the sweep's subjects and Work vs Billing. `assignedQuarterStart` (domain): quarterly only, and **NEVER over quarters already billed** (0338's rule; a quarter recorded after the first bill leaves the service on the quarters it was billed on). `billingPeriods` (`usecase/billing/periods.ts`) is the one cut for the sweep, bill-early and Work vs Billing; such a period is called by its months ("Feb-Apr 2026") and is billed on its last day, never before the schedule's first invoice date (`billingDues({ onClientQuarter })`: the schedule was set on the company's quarters, so its first date is no anchor). Tests `tests/billing/model.test.ts`, `work-vs-billing.test.ts`, integration `vat-quarter`.
- **The go-live month (Q027, 0350).** `tenancy.tenant.auto_billing_since` (0321) is the first day of the month the firm chose when switching on (`PUT /billing/settings {autoBilling, autoBillingFrom: "YYYY-MM"}`; absent = this month; alone it moves the month while on, refused while off; at most twelve months ahead). Floor for everything automatic: `endsBeforeGoLive` stops the sweep writing a bill for a period that ENDS before it (a period across it is billed); final bills read it; Work vs Billing shows the NOTE `before_auto_billing` for work before it. Ledger `tenant.auto_billing_set` carries `startsOn`; `tenant.auto_billing_from_set` a move. Advances untouched.
- Dates guards (0321): an `on_date` before the first period moves forward by whole spans; a period that has not begun is never billed; a schedule's first invoice may not precede its first period; years 2000-2099.

## The advance and the final bill (auto-invoice phase 2, 0320)

A bill has a `kind`. Rules, each the firm's (Ashish) or the user's, 2026-10-01:

- **Every advance is a GST tax invoice.** The "Record advance" tick and route are GONE. Signing writes an ADVANCE BILL (`AdvanceBiller`, via the engagement use case's `AdvanceBilling` port; the daily billing queue catches up every firm's letters still owing one, WHATEVER the auto-billing switch says). A letter has ONE advance for the whole or one per service (`engagement_letter_line.advance_amount`, never on a grouped line), never both (`ONE_KIND_OF_ADVANCE`, `INVALID_LINE_ADVANCE`, and a trigger each way). The amount is BEFORE tax. An advance bill is raised on an invoice of its own and cannot be skipped (except where nothing waits on it, below).
- **The gate (`AdvanceGate`, Approve start, `MoneyMovedListener`, `recordAdvance`) was REMOVED in 0322**: nothing waits on the money.
- **The final bill** of a one-time service (or fee group) is written when its job is DONE (orchestrator `jobDone` port; the daily queue catches up), dated the day it finished, at the letter's price. A service with no process has a job that never finishes, so **Bill it now** writes it today. Finals follow the firm's switch; advances do not. Finals are written automatically only for work finished on/after `tenant.auto_billing_since` (set on every switch-on; backfilled to the deploy day) and never for an engagement a hand-raised line already bills (switching on once billed every audit ever finished).
- **FIFO, before tax.** `crm.advance_pool` (view) = what is left of each advance on an ISSUED invoice: its line's net, less credit notes at pre-tax share, less what bills took (counted while raised or covered). A period/final bill takes from the service's own advance, else the letter's, earliest first, and the line bills the BALANCE in words naming the fee, the advance and its invoice. A bill the advance pays in full gets NO invoice (`covered`: even a zero invoice draws a gapless number). The take is re-checked in the write under `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on the advance invoice.
- **The refund.** Once everything an advance covered is finished (ended with last period billed, or one-time with final bill raised/covered/skipped) and no bill of it is open, the remainder is on "To refund": a credit note on the advance invoice, reason `advance_refund`, with its share of tax (SQL, capped at what the invoice carries). The money goes back as a negative payment. A firm may keep it, with a reason.
- `crm.billing_due` names an engagement letter, so it is the fourth entry in the signed-letter freeze test's `NOT_TERMS` (what the letter CAUSED, written after the signature).

## The advance starts the work, and is chased (0322, 2026-10-02)

Ashish changed the rule (verbatim in `bib/prompts/auto-invoice/NEXT-SESSION.md`): the advance invoice is created as a DRAFT and the jobs start at once; SPOC, Key Manager and partner are reminded on the dashboard while it is draft or unpaid; weekly it escalates to the CEO/COO by notification and email ("generally it comes within 5 to 15 days of starting of work").

- **Signing = every engagement `active` + the advance DRAFTED** (`EngagementService.sign` → `AdvanceBiller.billAdvances`; DocuSign, recorded acceptance and the import all go through `sign`). Draft tax rate: the last rate invoiced on that work, else the ISSUING COMPANY's default (`crm.issuer_tax_default`, Your firm); neither = no draft, the bill waits under To raise with `draftBlocked: no_tax_rate`, never a guessed 0%. Drafting draws no number.
- **Catch-up at deploy** (`AdvanceBiller.catchUp`, daily billing queue): letters left in `pending_advance` start their work (`startWaitingWork`, ledger `engagement_letter.work_started_before_advance`) and get a draft, EXCEPT where an issued invoice already bills that work on no bill (`handRaised`, told apart by two instants, never a zone-less day): no draft, an admin Links it.
- **Where an advance stands** (`advanceStage`, pure): `not_raised` (no invoice, or draft cancelled) · `on_draft` · `unpaid` · `part_paid` · `paid` (money arrived and nothing owed; a credit note zeroing an unpaid advance is `unpaid`, not `paid`, 0321). `advanceReminder`: pending until paid; `pendingSince` = signing (no invoice/draft) or issue (unpaid); **Overdue** at the firm's `advance_draft_overdue_days` (3) or `advance_unpaid_overdue_days` (15); `overdueOn` is the day My day dates the row by; **escalated** 7 days after signing (`ADVANCE_ESCALATE_AFTER_DAYS`).
- **Who is reminded** (`AdvanceDesk`, `advance-reminders.ts`): the client's SPOCs for the advance's Depts (in force, active staff), its Key Manager, and the PARTNER = first person up the Key Manager's `reports_to` chain whose grade rank is at least `advance_partner_min_rank` (9, the default ladder's Director; recursive CTE, depth 20). `GET /billing/advances?mine=true` returns the pending advances the caller chases, each with `duties` (`spoc`/`key_manager`/`partner`/`admin`); an admin gets those waiting for an admin (`not_raised`, `on_draft`) or escalated.
- **Weekly summary** (`AdvanceDigest`): queue `billing.advance-digest` on `BIB_WORKER_ADVANCE_DIGEST_CRON` (hourly); due ON the firm's `advance_digest_weekday` from `advance_digest_hour` in ITS zone (`digestDueOn`), never caught up another day. The week is CLAIMED first (`crm.advance_digest`, one row per firm per week), then ONE email per admin (`is_administering_role`) listing advances pending 7+ days after signing; a reader who may not see fees gets no amounts. Ledger `tenant.advance_digest_sent`. Sent through `SmtpMailer` (`setup/mailer.ts`): the worker needs SMTP settings too (Terraform `run_worker.tf` + `iam.tf`, DEPLOY NOTE for Anish).
- **Settings** (`GET/PUT /billing/settings`, PUT `tenant:admin`), partial body: `autoBilling`, `advancePolicy` (whole policy, `advancePolicyProblem`; ledger `tenant.advance_policy_set`), `taxDefaults` (0-100; a company left out keeps its rate; ledger `tenant.tax_default_set`).
- Client dunning is unchanged (an issued advance invoice is chased like any). A one-time service whose jobs were ALL cancelled is billed like one with no process, and a cancelled job never holds a final bill back; `/billing/dues` applies `mine` and the default status (`pending` = open or drafted; `all` = history) IN SQL before `LIMIT 5000`, and leaves advances out of `mine`.

## Billing safeguards (0321, 2026-10-02)

Ten defects found in 0318-0320 by three review agents; all fixed with tests.

- **A bill is CLAIMED before it is settled** (`billing_due.claimed_at`, 2 minutes then free). Raise claims its bills AND the advance bills they take from, re-reads, then draws the number; link, skip, refund and keep claim too. A second request gets `BILL_BUSY` (409) with nothing numbered. Closed: two raises of one bill = two invoices; two raises spending one advance; a double-click refund crediting twice.
- **Link**: never onto another bill's ADVANCE invoice (that money is the advance); an advance bill is linked only by `tenant:admin` (`ADVANCE_LINK_NEEDS_ADMIN`, 403) to an invoice no other bill is on (a paid invoice starts the work; an associate once linked an old paid invoice, started work with no advance and created a phantom advance).
- **An advance raised again after its invoice was cancelled** moves the bills that took from it onto the new invoice (`markInvoiced`, same transaction): the same advance came off twice. `OPEN_DUE_SQL` matches the read (a covered bill is open once its advance invoice is not issued), so raising no longer drew an orphan number.
- **An advance nothing waits on may be skipped** (`advanceHoldsWork`); refunds carry `paid` so the screen pays back only what was received.
- **Raise amounts**: above zero; a priced bill billed at another figure only by a login that sees fees (`PRICE_NOT_VISIBLE`, 403), both figures in the `billing_due.invoiced` payload. **No amounts in invoice line text** ("…, less advance (INV/…)"): descriptions reach logins the fee axis hides prices from.
- **Skipping is a permission**, `billing:skip` (Partner and Manager by default; 0321 re-publishes untouched default roles), the user's call: "permission bana do iski and jispe woh hogi woh kr skata with a reason".

## The ad-hoc advance: money taken before the letter (Q044-Q048, 0363-0364, 2026-10-05)

Ashish: in 5-10% of cases the advance comes BEFORE the engagement letter. It is raised as a tax invoice and sits on "Advances to be adjusted" until mapped to the work it pays for. Design: `bib/prompts/build-2026-10-05/S4-advance/DESIGN.md`.

- **Tables (0363).** `crm.adhoc_advance` (one per advance: invoice, raiser, note; the invoice's NET is the advance, read never copied); `crm.advance_allocation` (one row per map: a service and optionally one job, or a whole signed letter; append and END, never erased: ending keeps what bills took in `kept_amount` and frees the rest; a trigger lets only the end columns move); `crm.engagement_letter_advance` (what a DRAFT letter names as received; frozen with the letter); `billing_due.advance_allocation_id`. RLS on all, composite keys; a trigger refuses a target of another client, company or currency for every writer.
- **The pool learns `source`:** `crm.advance_pool` is `letter_bill` (0320 rows, numbers unchanged), `allocation` (effective amount less what bills took) and `unmapped` (net less credits less every allocation; NO bill ever takes from it). A bill's pick: the service's own advance, then allocations to this service (or its fee group, or the job whose period this bill's period holds), then the letter's own advance and letter-wide allocations, oldest first. `takeFromPool` re-checks the SOURCE under the advisory lock keyed on the advance INVOICE (map and end take it too). A bill still takes from ONE source.
- **Use case** `usecase/billing/adhoc.ts` (`AdhocAdvanceService`), port `AdhocAdvanceStore`, adapter `postgres/adhoc-advance.ts`, routes `api/handler/advance.ts`: `GET/POST /api/v1/advances`, `GET /advances/{id}`, `GET /advances/{id}/targets`, `POST /advances/{id}/allocations`, `POST …/allocations/{aid}/end`, `POST /advances/{id}/give-back`, `GET/PUT /engagement-letters/{id}/advances-received`. Raise = a DRAFT through `InvoiceDrafter.prepareDraft/prepareLine` (every invoice rule), one line "Advance, to be adjusted against services" (no amount in words), tax from the company default unless sent, manager = person's pick else client's Key Manager else the raiser; one transaction (B34's rule).
- **Refusals:** `ADVANCE_NOT_ISSUED`, `ADVANCE_OVER_MAPPED`, `ADVANCE_IN_ANOTHER_CURRENCY` (never converted), `ADVANCE_OF_ANOTHER_ISSUER` (in that company's tax books), `ADVANCE_OF_ANOTHER_CLIENT`, `ADVANCE_TARGET_NOT_LIVE`, `ADVANCE_JOB_NOT_OF_SERVICE`, `ALLOCATION_USED_UP`, `ALLOCATION_ENDED`, `FEES_NOT_VISIBLE`. `INVOICE_ADVANCE_MAPPED`: a mapped advance's invoice is not cancelled. A credit note on an ad-hoc advance may take only its UNMAPPED part (B06 cap ignores allocation rows); Give back is that credit note, reason `advance_refund`.
- **Permission `advance:manage`** (0364): raise, map, end; given to every role carrying `billing:skip` or `tenant:admin` (whatever the firm renamed its roles); defaults Partner and Manager. Give back is `invoice:issue`; reading `party:read`.
- **Q045 different managers:** each allocation freezes its manager at map time (the service's, by `billingManagerFor`; a letter-wide one, the client's Key Manager). `crm.invoice_manager_share` (invoice, manager, net share): ordinary invoice = one row; ad-hoc advance = one row per allocation's manager plus the rest to the invoice's manager. A re-map moves the share. Always through a service, never to a manager alone (user, 2026-10-05). **Superseded in part by Q085/0376** (`revenue-and-reports.md`: an advance is revenue only when a bill takes it).
- **Q048 scenario 1:** a draft letter names advances (this client's, company, currency, issued, at most what is unmapped); they are in `canonicalContent` (a `received` row each, ONLY when any) and the Annexure PDF prints "Advance received" under the fees. Signing (`EngagementService.sign`, every path) calls `NamedAdvanceMapping.mapNamedOnSigning`: an allocation to the LETTER for min(named, unmapped now), `how = letter_signed`, once (partial unique index); post-commit and swallowed, the daily queue catches up. The letter's own "advance required" is separate, further money (user, 2026-10-05).
- **Q047 reminder:** `usecase/notify/adhoc-reminders.ts` on `notify.advance-reminders`'s hourly cron: from the firm's `advance_unmapped_day` (1-28, default 1; `PUT /billing/settings unmappedReminderDay`, ledger `tenant.advance_unmapped_day_set`) at the daily email's hour in its zone, for the rest of that month, ONE `advance_unmapped` notification per person (each advance's raiser if still staff, every admin), dedupe per person per month; amounts per currency rendered at read time for a reader who sees fees; resolved when nothing of theirs is left.
- Ledger: `adhoc_advance.raised`, `advance_allocation.mapped` (`how`), `advance_allocation.ended` (`kept`, `freed`); reasons and notes stay on the rows. Walk `adv-walk.mjs` on `adv-fixture.mjs`.
