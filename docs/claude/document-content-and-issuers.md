# Document content and issuers

Scope: what a proposal or letter SAYS (prose blocks, governing law), the firm's companies as issuers, the Annexure letter and fee groups (0083), the proposal as a document (0084), what a document prints about its parties (0334), and who a commercial document may name (F-10-1, 0306). Rendering mechanics are `rendering-and-document-store.md`; the lifecycle is `proposals-letters-and-engagements.md`; invoices are `invoicing-and-gst.md`.

## What a document SAYS: prose and whose law (G1.7, G1.8)

- **Prose blocks are COPIED onto a document, never referenced.** `crm.standard_document_block` is what the firm maintains (exclusions, commercial notes, team block); `crm.document_block` is what one document says. A new document copies the standard set and owns those rows, so editing the standard changes the NEXT document, never one issued. A copy beats a pin here because a client negotiates an exclusion away.
- **One row per line, not a blob** (edited a line at a time, reordered, struck out); `is_heading` lets the team block be a table with headings. One table, two nullable parents and a CHECK; it inherits the signed-letter freeze since `crm.refuse_signed_child_change()` resolves its parent from `engagement_letter_id`.
- **`jurisdiction` on a standard line is load-bearing.** NULL prints everywhere; a code prints only on a document written for that country ("exclusive of GST" is wrong on a UAE proposal; the firm caught it in its own template). `blocksFor`: retired lines never copied, unscoped always, scoped only to its country, and an UNKNOWN country (`*`) takes only what is true everywhere.
- **Whose law a letter names is checked against the ISSUING ENTITY.** `ClauseBody` carries `governingLaw` and `courts`, each an ISO code AND the printed words (the code cannot print "laws of the State of Delaware"; the words cannot be compared). `addClause` refuses a term that is not the issuer's: the firm's real letter was issued by the US entity and named Indian law and Delhi courts, and a human review let it out. This is NOT the clause's `jurisdictions` field (where a clause may be USED); it says what the clause SAYS vs who contracts.
- **An unregistered issuer is refused too** (`seedFirmEntity` writes an organization with no `core.legal_entity`, so every default issuer has no jurisdiction, which is where the original letter was written from). Limit: a deliberate foreign choice (English law under an Indian entity) is unrepresentable; making it representable belongs on the LETTER and nobody has decided it.

## Per-company prose, the firm's companies, one currency (T6.18, 0312, 0313)

- **Standard prose is per ISSUING COMPANY, inheriting a default.** `standard_document_block.issuing_entity_party_id` is null for the firm-wide set. `blocksFor` rule, PER KIND: if the issuing company has any ACTIVE line of a kind, its lines are the set for that kind; otherwise the defaults. Another company's lines never reach it; the `jurisdiction` filter applies inside the chosen set.
- Seeding passes the issuer: a PROPOSAL's country is the client's when known, else where the issuer is registered, else `*` (before, a UI proposal was always `*`, so country-scoped lines never reached it); a LETTER uses its issuer and own country.
- **Changing a draft's issuer replaces its prose with the new company's set** (D9), in the same transaction (`insertDocumentBlocks` / `strikeDocumentBlocks` exported from `block-repository.ts`), refused with `PROSE_EDITED` if a line was edited or client-specific unless `replaceEditedProse`. **A letter drafted from a quote carries the quote's prose** (D11), with only cover and acceptance from the standard set; a line changed on the quote comes across with no `sourceId`.
- **"One of the firm's companies" = `crm.is_firm_entity`** (the firm itself or a member). `/firm-entities` is the screen's list with registration, the countries routed to it and `defaultCurrency` from `defaultCurrencyFor` (IN INR, AE AED, SG SGD, US USD, else the reporting currency; ONE copy, the frontend keeps none). Creating one = organization + `core.legal_entity` + preference row for its own country in ONE transaction (`PostgresFirmEntityRepository`; a half-created company would sit in the CLIENT list). A country another company serves is refused (`COUNTRY_ALREADY_SERVED`); a registered country never moves (`JURISDICTION_FIXED`). `registered_address` is `{ "lines": [...], "region", "postalCode" }`, printed since 0334.
- **Membership is `crm.firm_entity` (0313), not routing** (`crm.firm_entity_preference`): one row per company the firm issues from; before it, re-pointing a company's last country took it off every list. Writers: 0313's backfill, `POST /firm-entities` (same transaction as the company), and a trigger making any party a routing row names a member (the older way in, used by `smoke.sh` and the fixtures). Never deleted (`retired_at` exists). `is_firm_entity` = the firm OR a live member.
- **One document, one issuer, one currency was kept deliberately (D1)**: lines are held to the document's currency by a composite key (0011). A DRAFT's currency can change while it quotes nothing (0312 made the line key `ON UPDATE CASCADE`; a line struck off a draft used to pin it); the service refuses a new currency over a live line (`CURRENCY_LOCKED_BY_LINES`). Totals are read PER BILLING FREQUENCY (`totalsByFrequency`; the PDF's Summary of Fees ends with the same figures). **Superseded in part by 0344:** a proposal or letter may now price lines in several currencies (CLAUDE.md, Money); the stored-rate rule holds.
- **An own company is never a CLIENT (2026-09-29).** Invoices refuse it (`BILLS_THE_FIRM`); every document naming a client refuses it with `CLIENT_IS_OWN_COMPANY` (400): proposal draft, letter draft (including from a quote, both via `draftLetter`), a lead capture whose dedup lands on one. `GET /parties` and `/parties/{id}` still return own companies with `isOwnCompany` (a task may name the firm); the list computes it as two joins, held to the function row for row by `tests/party/own-company.integration.test.ts`. Client screens hide them.
- **A draft LETTER changes in place (T6.18 S6)** like a proposal. `PATCH /engagement-letters/{id}`: the head (title, Background, addressee, dates, advance); currency while no line or section row exists at all (live or pulled; their keys do not cascade, 0017/0083); country and issuer while NO TERM is on it (`TERMS_ON_LETTER`: terms were checked against the old issuer's law and cannot be taken off). A new issuer/country replaces the wording in the same transaction (D9). `PATCH …/lines/{lineId}` changes a line with add rules judged on the line as it will be; a line a section pays for keeps no price. The read carries `issuer.jurisdiction`, `totalsByFrequency` and `document` (the PDF's own `planAnnexure` with each fee row's `lineId`/`feeGroupId`). The letter PDF is unchanged: its Summary of Fees prints no per-frequency total (a re-rendered annexure letter must read as the one the client holds).

## The Annexure letter, and one fee for several services (0083)

The firm's format (10X Global's MyDojo letter, July 2026): a **cover letter** (date, addressee, subject, paragraphs, "Yours sincerely", the firm's signature, an Acceptance and the client's), **Annexure 1** (Scope, Deliverables & Fees in SECTIONS with a Summary of Fees), **Annexure 2** (numbered terms).

- **Layout is a column on the letter; every letter drafted now is `annexure`** ("switch everyone", 2026-09-26). 0083 set every already sent/signed/withdrawn letter to `classic` (true of them). The layout is RECORDED, never derived from code version (a rendered letter's bytes are evidence). Frozen with the signed letter (0019); NOT a term (absent from `canonicalContent`).
- **The classic path is untouched and a test holds it.** `PdfLibRenderer.render` branches on `annexureLetter` after shared setup; `render-source.ts` on `layout` after the head query. `tests/render/layout.test.ts` PINS the hash of a classic letter and an invoice from before 0083. **If it fails, do not update the constant**: a moved hash is every issued document re-rendering differently.
- **A FEE GROUP is ONE fee for several services = a section of Annexure 1.** "USD 5,000 per month" for accounting, tax and payroll across four companies is TWELVE lines under G0.1; before 0083 the only way was twelve prices nobody agreed to. `crm.engagement_letter_fee_group`: title, amount PER BILLING PERIOD, NOT NULL frequency, payment terms ("40% advance, 60% on completion"), the Summary-of-Fees sentence, the section's prose. Its letter (A, B, …) is its RANK among live groups (`sectionCode`), so striking one never leaves a letter starting at B.
  - A grouped line has **no price of its own** (`el_line_grouped_is_unpriced_chk`: null amount, zero discount, no pricing note needed); a price on both would count twice.
  - Group held to the letter's currency and a line to its own letter's group by composite keys.
  - A group still pricing a live service cannot be struck off and a live service cannot sit in a struck-off group (`trg_el_fee_group_keeps_its_lines`, `trg_el_line_group_is_live`, named to sort after the freeze). A letter with an empty group cannot be sent (`FeeGroupEmpty`).
  - **It is a TERM.** `canonicalContent` appends one row per group (fee, words, POSITIONS of lines covered) ONLY WHEN THERE ARE ANY, adding no field to a line's row: DocuSign verifies against that string, so a letter sent before 0083 and signed after must render identically.
  - Totals count a group's fee ONCE. **MRR shares it** across covered engagements, the last taking the rounding (`crm.engagement_mrr`; an ungrouped line's arithmetic is 0038's). A grouped engagement bills at the GROUP's frequency. `billable-engagements` carries `feeGroup` so the invoice screen does not prefill a whole group's fee per engagement (four engagements of a 5,000 retainer = 5,000, not 20,000).
  - Lines enter a group at add (`feeGroupId`) or via `PUT …/lines/{lineId}/pricing` (in: gives up price, discount, note; out: needs a price or reason again, `LineNotPriced`).
- **Cover and acceptance are the firm's prose: block kinds `cover` and `acceptance`, LETTER-ONLY.** Legal text ("you may procure services … for your consolidated subsidiaries" binds a group), so the product never writes it. A proposal never gets them (`blocksFor`, `addBlock` refuses `LetterOnlyBlockKind`, `document_block_letter_only_kinds_chk`). **Nothing is seeded**: a firm with none prints salutation straight to "Yours sincerely".
- **What the Annexure prints that nothing typed:** restatements of terms already hashed and frozen: the ADVANCE (below the Summary), an out-of-pocket range, which companies a service is for, and who the client signs for ("Accepted for and on behalf of MyDojo, Inc., for itself and on behalf of …"). `planAnnexure` (`domain/engagement/annexure.ts`) decides structure, tested without a PDF: one item for one service across four companies (lines merge only when everything printed is the same), a company named once per section when every item covers the same ones and never on a one-company letter, own-priced lines as a last section.
- **Not built:** signatory's phone, page header, fee groups on a PROPOSAL (a quote's lines carry across ungrouped).

## The proposal as a document: "Proposal For Services" (0084)

Before 0084 nothing printed a proposal (`render-source.ts` refused the kind). It is its own document: a **cover** (the firm's full-page artwork with "Prepared for" and "Prepared by" over it), Background, "In-scope processes / services", SECTIONS by the firm's **Dept** ("A. Financial Reporting & Compliance"), each service under its **catalogue code** ("H3. Transfer pricing audit — Form 3CEB", "Fees H3: INR 50,000 Yearly"), a Summary of Fees (Section · Sub Section · Description · Frequency · Fees), Notes (team, exclusions, commercials), logo and reference on every page after the cover. `POST /documents/render` with `kind: "proposal"` files it like any render (not client-visible by default).

- **`planProposal` (`domain/proposal/document.ts`)** decides structure, tested without a page. A Dept is a section, in `service_axis` order, lettered by RANK among Depts used, from the PINNED version (a code with no row prints under the code; no Dept = last "Other services"); a service prints under its `ref` or is numbered (A1); a company is named only when lines are for more than one. **Not merged** (unlike the letter): two lines are two prices.
- **Columns:** `background` (carried to a letter drafted from the quote by the caller); `addressed_to_party_id` (a PERSON in the client's structure: `ADDRESSEE_NOT_A_PERSON` 400 / `ADDRESSEE_NOT_LINKED` 409); `prepared_by_user_id` (active STAFF; the key targets the GLOBAL account table, so the service holds it; set to the drafter; 0084 BACKFILLED from `proposal.drafted` actors, a fact not a default). `PATCH /proposals/{id}` revises a DRAFT's head (WHERE repeats `status = 'draft'`), records no event. `files.brand_asset.kind` gains **`proposal_cover`**, per issuing entity, PNG/JPEG only.
- **Artwork is the firm's; where the names go is the layout's.** The cover FILLS the page; blocks sit at fractions of the ARTWORK (`PREPARED_FOR_AT`, `PREPARED_BY_AT`), "for" in ink, "by" in white. No artwork = a plain cover (logo, "Proposal for Services", the title). Section headings use the template's deep red (`ACCENT`), a LAYOUT property, not a brand setting.
- **Prints that nothing typed:** template headings, "Our responsibilities:" (a multi-firm product cannot print "10x's Responsibilities"), restatements (each fee, out-of-pocket range, "valid until"). Money prints whole where whole ("INR 50,000"); frequencies in the firm's words ("Yearly").
- Letter and invoice paths untouched (flow engine took a `FlowStyle`, cursor a `turn` hook); the pinned hashes prove it.
- **Not built:** fee groups on a proposal, a serif body (the sample is Times; Noto Sans everywhere), page numbers.

## Who a document is from and to (audit 2026-10-03, 0334)

The "Tax Invoice" printed two names; letters printed "Registered in IN".

- **Held:** a firm company's registered address gains state + postal code; its tax registration (GSTIN in India, held to GSTIN shape; TRN UAE; EIN US; VAT elsewhere), PAN (India only), phone and email are the company's own PARTY IDENTIFIERS set from `PATCH /firm-entities/{id}` (each write voids the one in force; a value another party holds is refused, writing nothing). A client has `core.party.billing_address` (`PATCH /parties/{id}`, CA#22). Same jsonb shape both sides (`domain/party/address.ts`).
- **One reading:** `crm.print_identity(party, day)`: name, address (billing else registered), country, identifiers in force, website.
- **Frozen when it goes out:** `printed_parties` on `crm.invoice` and `crm.engagement_letter` is written by the issuing statement from that reading on the firm's day; a trigger refuses any other write. A draft reads live; NULL on a document already out = the print before 0334.
- **Prints:** invoice From / Billed to blocks (address, state and country BY NAME, GSTIN or country tax number, PAN, phone, email), total in words ("Indian Rupees ... Only", lakhs/crores); the letter's letterhead under the company name; the proposal's at the foot of every page after the cover.
- **Not printed, waiting on Sid:** bank details, LUT, e-invoicing (CGST/SGST/IGST, SAC and place of supply landed with S5, `invoicing-and-gst.md`). A credit note has no PDF yet.
- **Documents cluster (same audit):** a draft (`draft_copy`, or a copy drawn while a draft) is never shared (`DRAFT_NOT_SHAREABLE`, B10); a draft is dated on the firm's day in preview and draft copy alike (B42); PDFs are named "Invoice draft - <client>.pdf", "Invoice INV-2026-27-0001 - <client>.pdf" (CA#56); an Annexure letter with an empty cover page is not sent (`LETTER_HAS_NO_BODY`, in `send()` only, never in the verified hash, CA#2); Summary of Fees kept on one page with heading and total, and a 34pt logo (CA#60).

## Who a commercial document names: the client, not merely the firm (F-10-1, 0306)

Composite `(id, tenant_id)` keys prove a reference is THIS FIRM's, nothing more. The 2026-09-23 audit found: an invoice's `entity_party_id` could name a CLIENT (opening a gapless GST series in its name), its `party_id` the firm itself, a line's `engagement_id` another client's engagement (its scope reaching this client's portal), a letter's `proposal_id` another client's quote, a letter "issued by" a client, a letter line for another client's company, a letter addressed to another client's director. All refused now, before any number is drawn:

- **Firm company = `crm.is_firm_entity`** (0306; the list the "Charged by" picker shows). `DocumentService.isFirmEntity` asks the function; invoicing, proposals and letters ask `DocumentService`. The issuer must be one; the billed party must not (inter-company billing refused, Shrestha 2026-09-25).
- **The client's structure** = `RelationshipRepository.structureOf`: the client, every company under it by `parent_of` to any depth, every company it is `shareholder_of`/`director_of` (and what is under those), and people holding `director_of`/`authorised_signatory_of`/`employee_of`/`shareholder_of` in any; in force on the FIRM's day, archived left out, a recorded loop ends the walk. A letter line must be for one of those companies, an addressee one of those people. **The same read is `GET /parties/{id}/structure`**, so the pickers and the refusals are one definition. The fix for either refusal is to record the relationship on the client's page (409, like `QUOTE_NAMES_NO_ENTITY`).
- **An invoice's engagement is the billed client's** (`engagement.party_id = invoice.party_id`, what `billable-engagements` offers); **a letter's quote is its own client's**; **an archived client gains no new invoice** (0305).
- **The company that signed the letter charges for it** (2026-09-28): the invoice number is in its issuer's series, so billing another company's contract booked the sale in the wrong GST books for good. `addLine` refuses (`ENGAGEMENT_OF_ANOTHER_ISSUER`, 409: the answer is another invoice); `billable-engagements` carries `issuingEntityPartyId`/`issuingEntityName` so the raise dialog preselects "Charged by".
- **In the currency its letter priced it in** (2026-09-28, `ENGAGEMENT_IN_ANOTHER_CURRENCY`, 409, after the issuer check): a line takes the INVOICE's currency, so USD 3,000 on an INR invoice billed as ₹3,000. One company signs in several currencies (an Indian company billing an export client in USD).
- **The invariant ones are also WALLS (0306)**: invoice issuer and billed party, its series, its line's engagement, the letter's quote. Each trigger is NAMED to sort after the freeze (BEFORE triggers fire in name order; a change to a signed letter / issued invoice must be refused as that). **The structure is deliberately NOT a trigger**: relationships are effective-dated and back-dated, so a wall would judge a signed letter by a structure that changed later. A quote's or letter's issuer is not walled either (ten integration fixtures write them by hand; only the invoice's issuer opens a legal series).
