# Revenue and reports

Scope: the `/dashboard` revenue figures (by company, by manager, the Revenue report by manager or originator), the Work vs Billing read, and when an advance counts as revenue (Q085). All sit behind the fee axis. Billing mechanics: `billing-and-advances.md`; invoice rules: `invoicing-and-gst.md`.

Shared definitions (one statement; proven to add up to the paisa between reports):

- **BILLED / INVOICED** = ISSUED invoices only, BEFORE TAX (`invoice_totals.net`, after discount), less credit notes issued in the period at their PRE-TAX SHARE of the invoice they credit (`amount × net / total`; a credit note reverses tax in proportion, so its whole amount off a pre-tax figure takes tax off twice). A credit note on an invoice cancelled since goes with it. Cancelled invoices and drafts are dropped everywhere.
- Each document rounded to four places after conversion (as `crm.receivable_ageing`), summed in SQL on `numeric`.
- **COLLECTED** = CASH: payments by `received_on`, WITH tax, at the invoice's stored rate; a bounce or refund is a negative payment and comes off its own month.
- **OUTSTANDING** = owed NOW from `crm.receivable_ageing`; a state, not cut by month.

## Billing by company (Wave 5, 2026-09-29)

`GET /api/v1/dashboard/revenue/by-company?months=12` (1-24), no migration: what each of the firm's companies billed per month, one small area chart per company (they bill in different currencies and cannot share an axis).

- **Window = the FIRM's months** (`billingWindow(dayInZone(now, firm zone), n)`); `issued_on` is a `date`, so no zone is needed for a document's month.
- **A company's currency is D1's table** (`defaultCurrencyFor`: IN INR, AE AED, SG SGD, US USD, else the reporting currency). Its `billed` counts invoices in that currency, or ALL at stored rates when that currency IS the reporting one. An invoice in a third currency is left out of `billed` and counted in `otherCurrencyInvoices` (no rate is stored between two non-reporting currencies and none is invented). `billedReporting` and the whole-firm `total` take every document at its stored rate.
- **Which companies**: `crm.is_firm_entity` and not archived, the firm first, PLUS any party that issued an invoice in the window (money billed by a company put away since is not dropped). A month with no row is `"0"`.

## Revenue by manager (auto-invoice T4, 2026-10-02)

Ashish: "invoices come under the manager's name and revenue is tracked by manager". Manager = `crm.invoice.manager_user_id` (0318), from the bills when raised or picked by a person, **frozen at issue**. `GET /api/v1/dashboard/revenue/by-manager?months=12`.

- BILLED = by-company's definition grouped by manager (a month's managers add up to the `by-company` total to the paisa). COLLECTED (user asked for it; industry-standard cash). OUTSTANDING from `receivable_ageing`.
- **Everything in the reporting currency**, each document at its own stored rate; no rate is looked up today.
- **One row names nobody** (`userId: null`): invoices before 0318, a hand-raised one with none, a bill that found none. Listed so the table adds up; no backfill (frozen; a guess would be wrong).
- **"Manager" is whoever invoices name** (a firm's ladder is its own config; never "ranks 5 to 8"). `activeStaff: false` keeps a leaver's revenue under their name. Names come FROM the membership (behind the wall), never the global `user_account`.
- **`financialYearStartMonth`** rides along (compliance policy's; null unset) so the screen offers "this financial year" in any country.
- **A manager a PERSON names is checked** (`MANAGER_NOT_STAFF`, 409): the column's key reaches the global `user_account`, so another firm's user could otherwise be credited. The raise dialog sends `managerUserId` (preselected to the client's Key Manager); a draft's manager changes with `PUT /invoices/{id}/manager` (`invoice.manager_set`; issued: `INVOICE_MANAGER_FROZEN`, 409). The billing desk passes `managerFromBills: true` and is NOT checked (resolved from the firm's own SPOCs; one who left since still earned that period, refusing would stick the bill).

## Who won the work: revenue by manager or originator (Q049, 0355, 2026-10-05)

Ashish (4 Oct): manager-wise or salesperson-wise? MANAGER is on every invoice (frozen at issue). The SALESPERSON is the letter's **originator**, one per letter (user approved 2026-10-05).

- **`crm.letter_originator`** (0355) is a SIDE table, not a letter column: a signed letter is frozen and who sold the work is not a term, so it stays correctable after signature (the fifth `NOT_TERMS` entry in the freeze test). No row = read off the deal; a row = a partner's word (`originator_user_id` NULL = "nobody", e.g. a renewal). Only its person moves (column GRANT); a deleted draft's row is final (0087's trigger). **`crm.letter_originator_in_force`** (security_invoker view): the person set, else the lead's owner (letter → proposal → lead), else who prepared the proposal; `how` = `set` | `lead_owner` | `proposal_preparer` | `none`.
- `GET/PUT /api/v1/engagement-letters/{id}/originator` (`usecase/originator`). PUT is `tenant:admin` (default Partner holds it; no new permission, so no role-seed republish to collide with other branches), active staff only (`ORIGINATOR_NOT_STAFF`, 409), `LETTER_DELETED`; ledger `engagement_letter.originator_set` (new and previous person, `previousHow`); same person again records nothing. Originator revenue is read THROUGH the letter, so a correction re-credits its invoices (unlike the manager).
- **`GET /api/v1/dashboard/revenue/report`** (`by=manager|originator`, `from`, `to`, `issuerPartyId`, `currency`). OUTSTANDING = owed at the close of `asOf` (`to`, or today if sooner) from `crm.receivable_ageing_on`. **Per CURRENCY, never converted or added across currencies** (GROUPING SETS). Default period: the firm's financial year to date, else twelve months; at most three years.
- **By originator, line by line**: a line's letter is its engagement's, else the letter of the bill it was raised from (a fee group's, a letter-wide advance's); each line takes its share of the invoice's credit notes (of its net), payments and balance (of its total) to four places, so one invoice carrying two letters' work credits two people and a currency's people add up to its total.

## An advance is revenue when a bill takes it (Q085, 0376, 2026-10-05)

Ashish: "Invoice to be mapped to manager, not at advance stage". `crm.invoice_manager_share` puts an advance's net on nobody (ad-hoc ones whole; a letter's advance line moved off its invoice's manager) and, for each bill that took part of it (`billing_due.advance_applied`), moves that part to the bill's manager on the day the bill counts (`counts_on`: its invoice's issue date, or the bill's date when the advance covered it). Each invoice's rows still add up to its net. The mapping rows (`allocation`, `unmapped`) left the view. `crm.advance_invoice`: their money and balance are nobody's in the manager figures (dashboard by month, owed by manager, the Revenue report, whose nobody row reads "No manager yet (incl. advances not adjusted)"). The originator view is unchanged (advances count when invoiced; S4's mapped advance may want its own attribution, follow-up).

## Work vs Billing (Q041, 2026-10-04, no migration)

Ashish (COO, 4 Oct): *"job done but bill not generated, hume kyun nahi mil sakta system mein yeh?"* `GET /api/v1/billing/work-vs-billing` (`party:read`): one row per client × service × period with the jobs, the statutory filings under that service, the bill (status derived as the desk does), its invoice and what is owed (`crm.invoice_balance`). An unbilled-work (WIP) report for fixed-fee work with receivables beside it. Read model only (`usecase/billing/work-vs-billing.ts`: pure `assembleRows`, `expectedBill`; port `WorkBillingFacts`; adapter `postgres/work-billing.ts`).

- **A bill does not store its job; they meet by SUBJECT and PERIOD.** Subject is the sweep's (`e:<id>` an engagement priced alone, `g:<id>` a fee group). A period job sits on the row whose bill period holds the job's first day. A job with no bill gets the period and bill date the SWEEP would give it: `expectedBill` calls `periodsFrom` + `billingDues` with every bill date treated as come (nothing assumes "the 1st of next month"). The year cut is the sweep's: pinned on the engagement (0338), else the issuing company's (0337), else the firm's (with only the firm's, a company on its own year would show a quarter the sweep never bills). One-time work is one row (`o:`): job and final bill. An advance is its own row. A statutory filing (generated for the engagement, or of the same company with its rule linked to the service: CA#11's predicate) sits under the row whose period holds its last day and is NEVER flagged (its service's bill covers it).
- **Flags are codes, the screen writes the words.** `no_bill` (period over, work done, no bill; cause `no_schedule` / `auto_billing_off` / `no_fiscal_year` / `not_written`), `no_final_bill` (one-time done; `auto_billing_off` / `before_auto_billing` / `not_written`), `skipped` (reason, who, when), `not_raised` (bill date passed; `no_price` when the letter fixed none), `not_issued` (a draft more than `DRAFT_STALE_DAYS` = 7 past the bill date), `unpaid_overdue` (issued, owed, past due; no due date = due on its date, the Xero/Zoho "due on receipt" default). `days` ages each; the summary buckets 0-30 / 31-60 / 61-90 / 90+.
- **Not problems, written as notes:** a recurring bill raised while its period's job is still open (`raised_before_work_done`; Ashish, 1 Oct: a retainer's work is not complete before the invoice), a bill date not yet come (`bill_due_on`), the billing run's next pass (`billing_run_next`, a day of grace), a period before the client's `billing_from` (`before_billing_start`), one-time work billed by hand (`billed_by_hand`), a paused period (`not_served`).
- **Invoices raised by hand** (a line on the work, not cancelled, its invoice on no bill) ride on a `no_schedule` row as `handInvoices`: they inform, never clear the flag; the cure is setting billing up.
- **Manager** = the bill's, else the invoice's, else the client's Key Manager today (`managerFrom`); the filter applies after assembly. Window `from`/`to` (default twelve months ending the firm's today, max three years). **Fees**: amounts null without the fee axis (`feesVisible`); paid/part-paid/overdue days stay. 2,000 rows at most (`truncated`). Q042 moved the arrears bill date (`billing-and-advances.md`). The screen offers Download CSV.
